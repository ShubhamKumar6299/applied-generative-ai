# Generative AI - Fundamentals

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI refers to models that can generate new content by learning patterns from existing data.
Unlike traditional AI systems, it focuses on creation rather than just prediction or classification.

---

## Generative AI vs Discriminative AI

### Discriminative AI

* Works on labeled data
* Learns to differentiate between classes
* Maps each data point to a specific label

**Used to:**

* Differentiate
* Classify
* Identify patterns
* Draw conclusions

**Example:** Email spam filters

**Limitations:**

* Cannot generate new content
* Limited contextual understanding

---

### Generative AI

* Learns the underlying data distribution
* Can generate new and original content
* Mimics creative capabilities

---

## GPT

GPT (Generative Pretrained Transformer) is a generative model based on the Transformer architecture, designed to generate human-like text.

---

## Building Blocks of Generative AI

* GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)
* VAEs (Variational Autoencoders)
* Transformers
* Diffusion Models

---

## Foundation Models

Foundation models are large pretrained models that can be adapted or fine-tuned for specific tasks and applications.

---

## Capabilities of Generative AI

* Text generation
* Image generation
* Audio generation
* Video generation
* Code generation

---

## Data Capabilities

* Data generation
* Data augmentation
* Synthetic data creation

---

## Applications

* Virtual worlds
* AI avatars and digital personalities
* Use across multiple domains and industries

---

## Key Takeaway

Generative AI extends beyond prediction by enabling systems to generate, simulate, and create new content.
